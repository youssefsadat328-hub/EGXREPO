How does this EGX stock score?

Type a ticker or company name. You get four 0–100 scores: profit growth, how cheap it is, how far it is from speculators, and chart red flags. Scores compare each stock with the rest of the exchange.

    All stocks

    Sort by any column. Select a ticker to open its full breakdown.

    This is a screening aid, not investment advice. A high score does not mean a stock will go up, and a low score does not mean it will fall. Check the numbers yourself before you decide anything.

    How scores work: each measure is ranked against the other EGX stocks (a score of 50 is typical, 90 is better than 90% of them). Profit growth is judged after Egyptian inflation. Cheapness uses P/E against similar companies and against the stock's own history. A stock marked low confidence is missing data or had a data warning.
